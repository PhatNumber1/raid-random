Raid Random

Demo mode: all numbers are made up.

This wizard sets up Raid Random for you. It adds a dock to OBS with your live Twitch stats, and one button that raids a similar-sized streamer and then ends your stream.

You need OBS Studio and a Twitch account. It takes about two minutes.

Please close OBS before you continue.

The Raid Random network

Signing in adds you to the Raid Random network, so other members can raid you and you can raid them. The Raid Random website gets your channel name, when you are live, your category and a rough viewer count. When you use the raid button it also gets your exact viewer count, the channels that could be raided, and how the raid ended. Once, it checks with Twitch that it is really you, using your Twitch sign-in, which it does not keep. Signing out removes you again. Raids others sent you stay in their history for up to 90 days.

Setup

Raid Random is copied to a folder of your Windows account. You can change all of this later with the gear in the dock.

You can close this window. Raid Random keeps running in the background. To change something later, press the gear in the dock or open RaidRandom.exe again.
